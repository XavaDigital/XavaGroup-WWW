02 / Billing

Received an invoice or receipt from Xava Group?

You're in the right place. Xava Group handles invoicing, receipts and payment reminders for all of our brands. Your invoice will relate to a purchase or service from one of the businesses above: Xava Digital, BeastMode, Medals.co.nz or Review Us.

  • The invoice itself names the brand and describes the goods or services supplied.
  • Payments made to Xava Group are applied to your account with the relevant brand.
  • Not sure what a charge is for? Send us the invoice number and we'll sort it out.
